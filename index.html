Good to know

Frequently asked questions.

Everything to know before you land — visas, money, weather, and getting around.

Most nationalities receive a free 30-60 day visa exemption on arrival for tourism. Check the current allowance for your passport before you fly, and make sure your passport is valid for at least six months.

November to February is cool and dry — the sweet spot for temples and islands alike. March to May runs hot, and June to October brings monsoon rain in the south, though the north stays green and lush.

The Thai Baht (THB) is used everywhere. ATMs are widespread in cities, and cards are accepted at hotels and malls, but keep small cash for markets, street food, and rural areas.

Domestic flights connect major hubs in under two hours. For shorter hops, overnight trains, air-conditioned buses, and ferries to the islands are comfortable, scenic, and easy to book in advance.

Every tour on this page can be reserved with a request form — no payment is collected here. A local coordinator will confirm your date and share payment details directly.

Yes — Thailand is one of Southeast Asia's most visited and welcoming countries for solo travellers. Standard precautions apply: use licensed taxis or ride apps, watch your belongings in crowds, and keep copies of your documents.
